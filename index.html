About me

A steady hand in a busy plant.

I began my HR career in January 2011 at Sri Bhagirath Textiles in Kalmeshwar, Nagpur, and spent over eight years there, finishing as a Senior HR Executive.

Since August 2019 I’ve been Manager HR at Damodar Industries Limited, Amravati unit — looking after the full employee life cycle: recruitment and onboarding, welfare, grievances, committees, training, and the audits that keep a certified textile unit honest.

I’m at my best when the paperwork and the people side agree — when a worker’s concern is heard early, the record is clean, and the auditor finds nothing to argue with.

What I do

Four pillars of the HR desk.

The work spans the whole employee journey — from the first document on day one to the last feedback form after training.

01

HR Operations

The day-to-day engine of the department: records, payroll inputs, recruitment logistics and reports that leadership can rely on.

  • Employee records — physical and digital
  • Payroll data, leave, bonus and error resolution
  • Interviews, orientations and onboarding
02

Industrial Relations

A plant runs on trust. I keep welfare, safety and communication channels open so small issues never become big ones.

  • Canteen, drinking water, first aid, rest rooms, ambulance
  • Notices, grievance handling, plant liaison
  • Safety compliance with production, quality & security
03

Employee Relations

Structured forums where workmen are heard, and a pulse on the mood of the shop floor, inside and outside the plant.

  • Monthly HR meets with workmen
  • Grievance, transport, canteen & works committees
  • Prevention of sexual harassment committee
04

Training & Development

Training that starts from what departments actually need, and ends with feedback that reaches the HR head.

  • Training-needs analysis with department heads
  • Annual training calendar and programmes
  • Participant feedback and reporting

Social & compliance

Audit-ready, every single day.

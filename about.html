About me

The person behind the process.

Fifteen years of working with people, paperwork and production — and a firm belief that all three work better when HR is approachable.

Sanjay P. PandeyManager HR · Damodar Industries

I’m Sanjay Premnarayan Pandey, an HR professional from Nagpur, Maharashtra, with a career built entirely inside the textile industry.

I started in January 2011 as an HR executive at Sri Bhagirath Textiles Ltd. in Kalmeshwar. Over the next eight-plus years I worked through the fundamentals — employee records, payroll inputs, recruitment support, grievance handling, training coordination — and grew into the role of Senior HR Executive.

In August 2019 I joined Damodar Industries Limited at its Amravati unit as Manager HR. There the scope widened: I now look after the complete HR and industrial-relations function and the social & compliance audits that matter so much to export-oriented textile units.

Textile manufacturing is people-intensive and audit-heavy. That combination suits me — I like the human side of the job and I like a well-kept file just as much.
